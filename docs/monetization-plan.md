## PART 1: COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

### 1.1 ServiceTitan (Enterprise / Large Contractors)

**Pricing Model:** Per-technician, per-month subscription. No public pricing.

| Tier | Est. Cost/Tech/Month | Key Features |
|------|---------------------|--------------|
| **Starter** | $245-$300 | Dispatching, scheduling, work orders, basic invoicing |
| **Essentials** | $300-$400 | + Mobile estimates, custom reporting, inventory |
| **The Works** | $400-$500 | + Commission tracking, payroll, memberships, advanced reporting |

**Revenue Breakdown (public company):**
- Platform subscriptions: 71% of revenue
- FinTech/payment processing: 25% of revenue
- Professional services (implementation): 4% of revenue

**Add-ons (+30-50% to monthly bill):**
- Marketing Pro (automated campaigns, ROI tracking)
- Phones Pro (call tracking, recording)
- Pricebook Pro (dynamic pricing)
- Fleet Pro (GPS tracking)
- Buy Now Pay Later (Affirm partnership)

**Payment Processing:** Markup of 0.25%-0.75% on top of interchange.
**Implementation Fee:** $5,000-$50,000+ (one-time, non-refundable).
**Minimum viable cost:** ~$750/month for a 3-tech shop = $9,000/year before add-ons.

### 1.2 Procore (Enterprise / General Contractors & Commercial)

**Pricing Model:** Based on Annual Construction Volume (ACV), NOT per-user. Unlimited users included.

| Company Size | Est. Annual Cost |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Small contractor (<$5M ACV) | $4,500-$10,000/year |
| Mid-size GC ($5M-$50M ACV) | $10,000-$35,000/year |
| Large GC ($50M+ ACV) | $35,000-$60,000+/year |

**Key Modules (gated behind tiers):**
- Core: Project management, daily logs, documents, photos
- Preconstruction: Bidding, estimating, plan rooms
- Project Execution: RFIs, submittals, change orders
- Financial Management: Budgeting, contracts, invoicing
- Workforce Management: Time tracking, field productivity

## PART 3: VASCOAPP FREEMIUM PLAN

### 3.1 Tier Structure

---

#### GRATIS (Free Forever)
**Target:** Solo contractors just starting out.

- 1 user
- 5 active jobs
- 10 quotes/month, 10 invoices/month
- Basic scheduling & calendar
- 50 clients max
- 5 AI insights/month (taste of the 45 generators)
- Basic compliance checklists (1 country)
- "Powered by Vasco" on quotes/invoices
- Community support
- 500MB photo storage

**NOT included:** Payment processing, accounting integrations, e-invoicing, team members, automation packs, PDF/CSV export, EVE AI.

---

#### VAKMAN (Craftsman) — EUR 29/month (annual) | EUR 39/month (monthly)
**Target:** Active solo contractors running a real business.

Everything in Gratis, plus:
- Unlimited jobs, quotes & invoices
- No Vasco branding (white-label)
- Payment processing via Mollie (pass-through fees)
- 1 accounting integration (Moneybird, Xero, QuickBooks, etc.)
- Basic e-invoicing (1 format per country)
- 50 AI insights/month
- EVE AI basic (end-of-day summaries, quote follow-ups)
- 3 automation packs (choose from 7)
- PDF/CSV export
- 5GB photo storage
- Email support
- Job costing & profitability tracking

---

#### MEESTER (Master) — EUR 59/month (annual) | EUR 79/month (monthly)
**Target:** Busy contractors and small teams (2-5 people).

Everything in Vakman, plus:
- 3 team members included (+EUR 19/user/month extra)
- Unlimited AI insights (all 45 generators)
- EVE AI full suite (all 7 automation packs)
- ML predictions (quote win probability, payment timing, duration)
- All 19 accounting integrations
- All e-invoicing formats (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X, Facturae, FatturaPA, Peppol)
- Cross-contractor benchmarking
- EU6 price index access
- Invoice photo scanning (pricing moat)
- Advanced P&L reports
- Priority support (chat + email)
- 25GB photo storage
- Client portal

---

#### AANNEMER (GC/Enterprise) — EUR 99/month (annual) | EUR 129/month (monthly)
**Target:** Aannemers, multi-trade project managers, growing companies.

Everything in Meester, plus:
- 10 team members included (+EUR 15/user/month extra — volume discount)
- Multi-trade project management
- Subcontractor management & portal
- Worker portal (schedules, timesheets)
- Site lead accounts
- Purchase order management
- Recurring job automation
- Calendar sync (Google, Outlook)
- Dedicated account manager + phone support
- 100GB photo storage
- API access
- White-label client communications
- Cohort analytics & business intelligence

### 3.6 Referral & Backlinking: "Bouw Samen" (Build Together)

**Contractor-to-Contractor:**
- Referrer: 1 month free on current plan
- Referred: 1 month free Vakman
- If referred upgrades within 90 days: referrer gets another month free
- Cap: 6 free months/year per referrer

**Aannemer-to-Subcontractor Network Flywheel:**
- Aannemers invite subs to Vasco (Gratis tier) for free
- If sub upgrades to Vakman: aannemer gets EUR 10/month credit per active sub (up to EUR 50/mo)
- Creates powerful network effect: GCs pull entire subcontractor networks

**"Powered by Vasco" Backlinks:**
- Free tier: "Powered by Vasco" on all quotes/invoices with link to vascobuild.com
- Vakman+: can remove branding
- Every document = organic SEO + brand exposure

**Trade Association Partnerships:**
- Techniek Nederland, ZVEH (Germany), FFB (France)
- Members get 20% off annual plans
- Vasco gets co-branded credibility

### 3.10 Key Strategic Principles

1. **Free tier is the moat.** Nobody else offers it. Every ZZP'er, Handwerker, and auto-entrepreneur starts free. Volume drives data + network effects.

2. **AI is the upgrade trigger.** 5 free insights create the "aha moment" — then the wall hits. Most natural conversion lever.

3. **Compliance is non-optional revenue.** EU regulations force upgrades. Contractors MUST comply. Vasco makes it easy.

4. **Payment processing is silent revenue.** Like ServiceTitan (25% from FinTech), every invoice paid through Vasco generates margin.

5. **Network effects through Aannemers.** One aannemer pulling 20 subs onto the platform creates a sticky ecosystem.

6. **Land in NL, expand to EU6.** Netherlands has highest ZZP density in EU. Prove model, then country by country.
