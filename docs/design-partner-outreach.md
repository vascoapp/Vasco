# Design-Partner Outreach + Pricing Discovery

Drafts for the "meantime" GTM work. Goal: **5 real NL solo contractors on
TestFlight**, and **5 pricing conversations**. Everything else is secondary to
watching real contractors use the core loop (see `launch-smoke-test.md`).

---

## Outreach message (NL — WhatsApp / DM, warm)

> Hoi [naam], ik bouw **Vasco** — een app die de administratie voor
> zzp'ers in de bouw grotendeels automatiseert: offerte maken, factuur
> sturen, betaling volgen, btw klaarzetten. Foto van een klus → offerte in
> een paar tikken.
>
> Ik zoek 5 vakmensen die 'm de komende weken als eerste testen en me eerlijk
> vertellen wat waardeloos is. Gratis, en je houdt inspraak op wat we bouwen.
> Zou jij een van die vijf willen zijn? Dan stuur ik je vandaag een TestFlight-link.

## Outreach message (EN version)

> Hi [name], I'm building **Vasco** — an app that automates most of the
> admin for solo trades: quote, invoice, chase payment, prep VAT. Snap a
> photo of a job → quote in a few taps.
>
> I'm looking for 5 tradespeople to be first testers over the next few weeks
> and tell me honestly what's useless. Free, and you get a say in what we
> build. Want to be one of the five? I'll send you a TestFlight link today.

**Where to find them:** existing network first (warmest converts), then
NL trade Facebook groups / ZZP forums, local trade suppliers, a bouwmarkt
noticeboard. 5 warm > 50 cold.

---

## The 15-minute discovery call (do NOT pitch — listen)

Run this with each design partner **before** they see the app. You're testing
demand + pricing, not demoing.

**Current-state (their world today):**
1. Walk me through the last quote you sent — from the call to sending it. How long did it take?
2. What do you use today for quotes / invoices / chasing payment? (Excel? WhatsApp? Jobber? Nothing?)
3. Last time a customer paid late — what did you do about it?
4. How much time per week goes to admin instead of on the tools?

**Pain ranking (don't lead the witness):**
5. If you could delete one admin task forever, which?
6. What's the most annoying part of getting paid?

**Willingness to pay (the number that de-risks the model):**
7. What do you pay today for any of these tools? (anchor)
8. If an app saved you [their answer to Q4] per week, what would that be worth per month to you?
9. *(after they see it)* At €39/month, is that a no-brainer, a maybe, or a no? Why?

**Rule:** if 4 of 5 can't name a tool they pay for today and stall on Q8, the
€39–69 tiers are a hypothesis, not a plan — revisit pricing before launch.

---

## What to watch during their first session (silent observation)

Give them the TestFlight link and the task: *"Send yourself a quote and turn it
into a paid invoice."* Then say nothing. Log:
- Where they hesitate / tap the wrong thing (onboarding, quote builder, send).
- The first thing they say out loud.
- Whether they reach "paid" without asking you.
- Did they get the confirmation email? (step 2 of the smoke test — activation killer if not.)

Their friction map IS your pre-launch backlog. One real session beats a week of
internal opinions.
