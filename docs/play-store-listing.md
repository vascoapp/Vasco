# Google Play store listing — copy for all six markets

Paste-ready. Play enforces **30 chars** for the app name, **80** for the short
description and **4,000** for the full description; every string below is inside
those limits (counts verified by `scripts/check-store-listing.mjs`).

## ⚠️ What this copy deliberately does NOT claim

`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and `MOONSHOT_API_KEY` are **not set in production**, so
`chat()` throws on line one for every task. Photo-to-quote, the 45 intelligence
generators, quote drafting and material extraction are all dark.

So none of this copy mentions AI, photo scanning, or automatic pricing.
Advertising a feature that throws is a Play policy problem — "the app does not
function as described" — as well as a rotten first impression for the one German
Handwerker we are trying to win. **When the LLM key is set, revisit this file:**
photo-to-quote is the strongest differentiator here and it is being left on the
table on purpose, not by oversight.

The e-invoicing claims are safe: those are deterministic format generators with
no LLM in the path, and the XRechnung output was fixed and validated in 2026-08.

### en

Vasco is the admin side of a trade business, built for the phone in your pocket rather than the laptop you never open.

Write a quote on site, turn it into a job, log your hours and materials as you go, and send the invoice before you have left the driveway. When it goes unpaid, Vasco chases it for you.

**Quotes that become invoices**
Build a quote from your own pricebook, send it, and let the customer accept it on their phone — no app, no account. An accepted quote becomes a job with the agreed scope already attached, so nothing is retyped and nothing drifts.

**Getting paid, without the awkward phone call**
Payment reminders go out on a schedule you set, escalating politely. You approve each one with a tap; Vasco never emails your customer behind your back.

**Built for European invoicing rules**
XRechnung and ZUGFeRD for Germany, Factur-X for France, Facturae for Spain, FatturaPA for Italy, Peppol across the EU. Correct VAT for six countries, including reduced rates and the reverse-charge cases that catch people out.

**The rest of the paperwork**
Time tracking, expenses, materials, customer records, and a handover pack at the end of the job. Connects to Moneybird, DATEV, Lexoffice, SevDesk, Pennylane, Holded, Fatture in Cloud, Xero and QuickBooks.

**Works where you work**
Basements and lofts do not have signal. Vasco keeps working offline and syncs when you surface.

For plumbers, electricians, gas engineers, painters and carpenters — solo or running a crew. Available in English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish and Italian.

### Screenshots

`scripts/make-play-screenshots.sh` reframes the 6.9" App Store captures rather
than re-shooting: it crops the iOS status bar and pads to an exact 9:16 canvas
in the app's own background colour, so the padding is invisible. The script owns
the output directory — fastlane uploads whatever it finds there, so anything not
declared in `SHOTS` is deleted on every run.

Two of the four captures are deliberately **not** shipped:

- **`4_photo_to_quote`** — 🔴 policy, not taste. The screen is titled
  "KI-Angebot" and pitches photo→AI quote generation. This listing claims no AI
  because `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` / `MOONSHOT_API_KEY` are unset in production and
  that flow throws; a screenshot makes the same claim to the same reviewer as
  the copy does. It ships when a provider key is funded.
- **`2_quote_builder`** — an empty-state capture. A new quote has no line items,
  so ~60% of the frame is blank and it reads as an unfinished app. Re-shoot with
  items added and it is a good screenshot.

`npm run check:listing` enforces all of this: 2–8 per locale, 16:9 or 9:16,
320–3840px per side, and no filename matching a feature that is dark in
production. Decoy-proven against all three.

**Known cosmetic defect in the shipped Italian capture:** the VASCO ANALYST card
reads `€ 5200`, where it-IT convention (and the German capture) is `€ 5.200`.
`it-IT` is configured in `src/i18n/formatting.ts`, so some path is interpolating
a raw number instead of using the formatter. Fixing it requires a re-shoot.
