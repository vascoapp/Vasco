# Cookie Policy — Vasco

**Last updated:** March 24, 2026

## Summary

The Vasco mobile application does **not** use cookies, web beacons, or similar tracking technologies.

## Local Storage

The App stores data locally on your device using AsyncStorage (React Native's secure key-value storage). This is device-local storage, not browser cookies, and is necessary for the App to function. Stored data includes your preferences, business records, and offline cache.

## Third-Party Services

Third-party services integrated into the App (Supabase, Mollie) may use their own cookies when accessed through web views. Please refer to their respective cookie policies:

- Supabase: https://supabase.com/privacy
- Mollie: https://www.mollie.com/privacy
