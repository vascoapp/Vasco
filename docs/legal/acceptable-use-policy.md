# Acceptable Use Policy — Vasco

**Last updated:** March 24, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy outlines prohibited uses of the Vasco application.

## Prohibited Activities

You may not use Vasco to:

1. **Fraudulent billing** — Create invoices for work not performed or inflate amounts
2. **False credentials** — Enter fake certifications, licenses, or insurance details
3. **Harassment** — Send threatening or abusive messages through the App's share features
4. **Spam** — Send unsolicited bulk messages to customers via payment reminders or follow-ups
5. **Data harvesting** — Systematically extract customer data for resale or unauthorized marketing
6. **Impersonation** — Create accounts impersonating other contractors or businesses
7. **Illegal work** — Manage or schedule work that violates local building codes or labor laws
8. **Tax evasion** — Use the App's features to create false financial records
9. **Unauthorized access** — Attempt to access other users' data or bypass security measures
10. **Automated abuse** — Use bots or scripts to interact with the App or its APIs

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
- Warning notification
- Temporary account suspension
- Permanent account termination
- Reporting to relevant authorities (for illegal activities)

## Reporting

Report violations to: **abuse@vasco.dev**
