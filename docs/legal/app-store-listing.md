### Full Description

**Vasco is the AI-powered operating system for construction trades professionals.**

Stop juggling Excel, WhatsApp, and paper. Vasco manages your entire workflow — from lead to payment — with AI that prepares your work and learns your preferences.

**SMART QUOTES**
- Describe a job → AI suggests line items and pricing
- Three-tier quotes (Good/Better/Best) with one tap
- Photo scan → instant quote from supplier invoices
- Send with digital approval link — customer accepts online

**AI-POWERED AUTOMATION**
- Morning briefing: overdue invoices, expiring certs, schedule conflicts
- One-tap actions: send reminders, create invoices, follow up quotes
- 24 automation types that prepare work for you — you just approve
- The more you use it, the smarter it gets

**JOB MANAGEMENT**
- Drag-and-drop scheduling
- Unified clock-in timer across all screens
- Photo documentation (before/during/after)
- Material tracking and cost monitoring
- Audit trail: created → quoted → scheduled → completed → paid

**INVOICING & PAYMENTS**
- Auto-draft invoices when jobs complete
- Generate payment links (Mollie integration)
- Overdue reminders — automatically prepared, you approve
- Late fee calculation for 30+ day overdue
- Weekly batch invoicing (Fridays)

**PRICING INTELLIGENCE**
- Scan supplier invoices → get market price comparisons
- "You're paying 15% above market for copper pipes"
- Regional pricing data across 6 EU countries
- Material price trends and supplier recommendations

**COMPLIANCE**
- Certification tracking with expiry alerts
- Country-specific permit requirements (NL, DE, FR, ES, IT, UK)
- Insurance and license monitoring
- Government portal quick links

**MULTI-LANGUAGE**
- Full support: English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian
- Localized for 6 EU markets

**INTEGRATIONS**
- 19 accounting providers (Moneybird, DATEV, Lexoffice, Xero, QuickBooks, etc.)
- Mollie payments (iDEAL, credit card, SEPA, Klarna)
- E-invoicing (XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X, FatturaPA)
- Calendar export

**FOR SOLO CONTRACTORS & TEAMS**
- Solo mode: everything a one-person trade needs
- Aannemer mode: multi-trade project management with P&L
- Site lead mode: safety, quality, and crew management

Download Vasco and run your trade like a pro.
