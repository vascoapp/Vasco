EU5 + UK + NL COMPLIANCE BIBLE (DRAFT)
Scope: France, Spain, Italy, UK, Germany, Netherlands.
Audience: Construction and trades contractors (general building, electrical,
plumbing/HVAC, gas).
Disclaimer: This is a structured research summary, not legal advice. Many
requirements depend on trade, project type, and region. Always confirm with
local regulators and professional bodies before operating.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EU-WIDE BASELINE (APPLIES ACROSS THESE MARKETS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) Data protection and privacy:
   - GDPR applies to customer data (names, addresses, invoices, job photos).
   - Maintain lawful basis, data minimization, retention, security, and
     data-subject rights workflows.

2) VAT and invoicing:
   - VAT invoicing rules exist in every country (local required fields,
     sequential numbering, VAT ID, tax rates, dates, etc.).

3) Health and safety:
   - National H&S laws implement EU safety directives for workplaces.
   - Construction sites typically require a safety plan, risk assessment,
     and site coordination for multi-employer sites.

4) Trade regulation and certification:
   - Electrical and gas work are regulated in all countries with mandatory
     standards, licenses, or authorized installer schemes.

5) Insurance:
   - Public liability and employer liability (if employing staff) are common.
   - Some countries require special construction liability insurance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UNITED KINGDOM (UK)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Core laws and compliance:
- CDM 2015 (Construction Design and Management) establishes duties for
  clients, designers, principal designers, and contractors. Requires
  planning, coordination, and appropriate competence.
  Source: HSE CDM overview
  https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/cdm/2015/overview.htm

- CIS (Construction Industry Scheme): contractors must register if paying
  subcontractors; verify subcontractors; deduct/pay tax; file monthly returns.
  Source: GOV.UK CIS contractor requirements
  https://www.gov.uk/what-you-must-do-as-a-cis-contractor

Invoicing:
- Invoices must include identification, dates, description, amounts, VAT
  where applicable.
  Source: GOV.UK invoice requirements
  https://www.gov.uk/invoicing-and-taking-payment-from-customers/invoices-what-they-must-include

Electrical:
- Building Regulations Part P: certain domestic electrical work must be
  certified by a competent person scheme or notified to Building Control.
  Source: GOV.UK competent person scheme
  https://www.gov.uk/building-regulations-approval/use-a-competent-person-scheme

Gas:
- Gas work requires Gas Safe registration.
  Source: GOV.UK license for gas work
  https://www.gov.uk/find-licences/registration-to-carry-out-gas-work

Insurance:
- Employers Liability insurance required if employing staff (min GBP 5m).
  Source: GOV.UK employers liability insurance
  https://www.gov.uk/employers-liability-insurance

UK checklist:
[] Register CIS if paying subcontractors
[] CDM dutyholder roles and H&S documentation (construction phase plan)
[] VAT registration and invoice compliance
[] Part P compliance / competent person scheme for electrical
[] Gas Safe registration for gas work
[] Employers liability and public liability insurance

NETHERLANDS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Business registration:
- All new companies must register in the KVK Business Register.
  Source: Business.gov.nl (KVK registration)
  https://business.gov.nl/starting-your-business/registering-your-business/registration-at-the-netherlands-chamber-of-commerce-kvk/

Building regulations:
- Besluit bouwwerken leefomgeving (Bbl) sets rules for safety, health,
  usability, and sustainability for all construction and renovation.
  Source: Rijksoverheid (Bbl)
  https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/bouwregelgeving/besluit-bouwwerken-leefomgeving

Invoicing:
- Invoice data requirements defined by Belastingdienst.
  Source: Belastingdienst invoice requirements
  https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/vat/vat_in_the_netherlands/vat_administration/invoice_requirements

Safety certification (often client-required):
- VCA/SCC safety certificate often required for high-risk work.
  Source: Business.gov.nl VCA
  https://business.gov.nl/staff/health-and-safety/certificate-for-working-safely-vca/

Netherlands checklist:
[] KVK registration completed
[] Bbl compliance evidence and NEN standards where relevant
[] VAT invoice requirements met (BTW ID, dates, rates, etc.)
[] VCA certification if client or site requires it
[] Trade-specific certifications (electrical/gas) per sector

GERMANY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Crafts registration and licensing:
- Handwerksordnung (HwO) governs regulated trades; Handwerksrolle
  registration is required for Annex A crafts.
  Source: HwO law and Annex A list
  https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hwo/
  https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hwo/anlage_a.html

Construction site safety:
- Baustellenverordnung (BaustellV) regulates construction site safety,
  pre-notification, and safety/health plans.
  Source: BaustellV
  https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/baustellv/BJNR128300998.html

Invoicing:
- UStG Section 14 defines mandatory invoice fields and issuance rules.
  Source: UStG Section 14
  https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__14.html

Germany checklist:
[] Check if trade is in Annex A (Meisterpflicht)
[] Register in Handwerksrolle with local Handwerkskammer if required
[] BaustellV requirements (pre-notification and safety plan as applicable)
[] VAT invoice compliance per UStG 14
[] Trade-specific standards (e.g., DIN/VDE for electrical; DVGW for gas)

FRANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Construction liability insurance:
- Decennial liability insurance is mandatory for construction professionals.
  Source: Service-Public (garantie decennale)
  https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2034

Owner-side construction insurance:
- Damage insurance (dommages-ouvrage) is required by the project owner
  before work starts; relevant to your customer education.
  Source: Service-Public (dommages-ouvrage)
  https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2032

Invoicing:
- Mandatory invoice fields and special mentions; includes insurance
  references for artisans and new e-invoicing requirements.
  Source: Entreprendre Service-Public (invoice mentions)
  https://entreprendre.service-public.fr/vosdroits/F31808

Energy renovation certification:
- RGE label for energy renovation and renewable installations; required for
  customers to access certain subsidies.
  Source: Entreprendre Service-Public (RGE)
  https://entreprendre.service-public.fr/vosdroits/F32251?lang=en

France checklist:
[] Decennial liability insurance (attestation provided to client)
[] Invoice mandatory mentions + insurance details on quotes/invoices
[] RGE label if doing energy renovation or renewable install
[] Site safety plan and risk assessment per national H&S rules

TASK 2.1 ELECTRICAL MODULE (DEEP DIVE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: electrical installations, testing, and compliance evidence by country.
Output: app-ready checklist items + evidence docs to store.

UK (England focus; Wales/Scotland/N. Ireland differ):
- Building Regulations Part P (Approved Document P) applies to electrical
  safety in dwellings and explains notification, design, inspection, testing.
  Source: Approved Document P (GOV.UK)
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/electrical-safety-approved-document-p
- In rented homes, electrical installations must be inspected/tested at least
  every 5 years by a qualified person; EICR is standard report.
  Source: GOV.UK guidance on electrical safety standards
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/electrical-safety-standards-in-the-private-and-social-rented-sectors-guidance/electrical-safety-standards-in-the-private-and-social-rented-sectors-guidance
- Legal basis for rented sector standards: 2020 Regulations (England).
  https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2020/312/contents
Evidence to store:
[] Competent person scheme registration (if applicable)
[] Electrical Installation Certificate (EIC) / Minor Works Certificate
[] EICR report + remedial works confirmation (if rental)

Netherlands:
- Working with electricity requires compliance with the Working Conditions
  Act (Arbowet) and NEN standards; risk assessment and qualified staff.
  Source: Business.gov.nl (RVO)
  https://business.gov.nl/regulations/working-electricity/
- NEN 1010: low-voltage electrical installations standard (installation,
  expansion, adaptation).
  Source: NEN standard page
  https://www.nen.nl/en/nen-1010-2020-nl-272897
- NEN 3140: safe operation of electrical installations and equipment.
  Source: NEN standard page
  https://www.nen.nl/en/nen-3140-2011-a3-2019-en-281305
Evidence to store:
[] NEN 1010 design/installation compliance evidence
[] NEN 3140 work procedures + inspection logs
[] Risk assessment (RI&E)

Germany:
- Electrical trade (Elektrotechniker) is a regulated craft in HwO Annex A,
  requiring Handwerksrolle registration (Meister or equivalent).
  Source: HwO Annex A
  https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hwo/anlage_a.html
- Low-voltage installation standards use DIN VDE 0100 series.
  Source: VDE Verlag standard listing
  https://www.vde-verlag.de/standards/0100149/din-vde-0100-100-vde-0100-100-2002-08.html
Evidence to store:
[] Handwerksrolle registration proof
[] VDE 0100 compliance/testing documentation

France:
- NF C 15-100 is the core low-voltage installation standard (mandatory).
  Source: AFNOR standard listing
  https://www.boutique.afnor.org/fr-fr/norme/nf-c15100/installations-electriques-a-basse-tension-completee-avec-la-mise-a-jour-de-/fa005536/1556
- CONSUEL issues Attestation de Conformite before first energization.
  Source: CONSUEL official site
  https://www.consuel.com/
Evidence to store:
[] NF C 15-100 compliance evidence (design/test)
[] CONSUEL Attestation de Conformite (yellow/green/blue/violet as applicable)

Spain:
