Vasco is a business app for tradespeople (plumbers, electricians, painters,
carpenters) in the Netherlands, Germany, France, Spain, Italy and the UK:
quotes, invoices, jobs and customers in one place.

SIGN-IN: use the review account in the Sign-In Information above. It is a
real account on our live backend with a completed business profile (a Dutch
plumbing company, app language English) and one sample customer, so you land
directly in the app without onboarding.

SUGGESTED 3-MINUTE WALKTHROUGH:
  1. Sign in → the "Today" tab opens.
  2. "Customers" tab → the sample customer "Jan de Vries".
  3. "Money" tab → create a quote for that customer, add a line, save it.
  4. Open the quote → create the invoice → open the PDF (on euro invoices a
     bank-transfer QR code is printed beside the bank details).
  5. Profile → Legal → Export my data / Delete my account (GDPR, in-app).

Payment-provider connections (Mollie / Stripe) and accounting connections
are optional and use the contractor's OWN provider account; nothing is
charged by Vasco. You do not need to connect them to review the app.

PRIVACY: no ads, no third-party tracking, no App Tracking Transparency
prompt. Data is stored in the EU (Supabase, Frankfurt). Account deletion can
be started in the app and at https://admin.vascobuild.com/delete-account.
