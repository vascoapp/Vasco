# Cookie Policy — Vasco

**Last updated:** May 9, 2026

## Summary

The Vasco mobile application does **not** use cookies, web beacons, or similar tracking technologies.

## Local Storage

The App stores data locally on your device using AsyncStorage (React Native's secure key-value storage). This is device-local storage, not browser cookies, and is necessary for the App to function. Stored data includes your preferences, business records, offline cache, and on-device-only state such as workflow-pack mute decisions and notification dedupe keys.

## Third-Party Services

Third-party services integrated into the App (Supabase, Mollie, Stripe, Resend, Sentry, Anthropic, Expo) may use their own cookies when accessed through web views or hosted checkout pages. Please refer to their respective policies:

- Supabase: https://supabase.com/privacy
- Mollie: https://www.mollie.com/privacy
- Stripe: https://stripe.com/privacy
- Resend: https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy
- Sentry: https://sentry.io/privacy/
- Expo: https://expo.dev/privacy

## Companion Web Pages

Vasco operates a companion web admin at `admin.vascobuild.com` (used by us internally; not a customer-facing portal). The admin uses a single auth cookie for PIN-protected access. No marketing or analytics cookies are set.
